Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TAP FOOTBALL QUIZ 2026. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect any personal information. All game data, including your nickname, scores, and unlocked levels, is stored locally on your device using browser storage (LocalStorage). We do not have access to this data, and it is not transmitted to our servers.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the Google Gemini API to generate quiz questions. No personal data is sent to Google; only the category and difficulty level of the quiz are sent to generate content.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app is family-friendly and does not collect information from children. Since no data is collected, we are fully compliant with COPPA and GDPR-K regulations.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
